At the moment, I am a student at San Diego State University, majoring in Computer Engineering and minoring in Web Design. When will you be graduating from SDSU?

I will graduate around December of the Fall of 2026. If not, then around May of Spring 2027.

What do you have planned for after graduation?

My plan after graduation is rather simple: I simply want to get a job that pays well. Then, as time went on and everything settled down, I may buy a house, try various new foods, and take some time off to vacation somewhere I have never been before.

What was your favorite class you took at SDSU?

I don’t exactly have a favorite class, but the one I feel most rewarded and enjoyed was ComPe 160, my first coding class at SDSU, where I learned, if not most of my foundational knowledge about coding.
