My History at San Diego State

Hi! My name is Tuong Truong. Below is a snapshot of my journey through my time at SDSU, spanning six semesters from spring 2023 to Spring 2026. Where each of the semesters contains three pictures of my works during that semester, either from an Art class or a COMPE class.

SEMESTER 1

Spring 2023

My first semester, Spring 2023, was also the first semester I took two Art classes at the same time. Art 100 Drawing 1 and Art 240 Digital Media. The Art 100 final project is a black-and-white drawing depicting a future city, shown in the middle. Both the top and the bottom are my works in Art 240; the top one is actually a photo of myself, where I used Photoshop to make it look like a black-and-white drawing. The bottom picture is my first project in Art 240, where I basically made a thumbnail for whatever topic of my choosing. I decided to make a fake reviewer thumbnail about a movie, and the theme is sci-fi.

SEMESTER 2

Fall 2023

All the projects of Fall 2023 belong to Art 200 Drawing 2. The first picture is a project about a surreal world, basically using color to create one, and all I could think of is a doomsday scenario where someone stands at the top of a hill with countless swords on the ground. The middle is my final project, which is supposed to use three-dimensional materials and focus on a specific theme. So I chose to create a city model with randomly merging colors that represent pollution. The last picture is actually a group project collage, where my group combines each of our individual artworks to create something new.

SEMESTER 3

Spring 2024

The Spring 2024 semester was when I took Art 107, which is Photography & Visual Communication. Surprisingly, that is one of, if not the most unique, art classes I have ever taken at SDSU. That class is mostly about taking photos around my environment and modifying them as needed. Case in point: the first picture shows me taking multiple shots of my chair and collaging them into a single chair, which looks very cool. The next two actually come from the same project: the middle one is where I experimented with inverse colors, while the bottom one is more of an accident; I did not expect it to turn out so well that it became my best work yet.

SEMESTER 4

Fall 2024

Fall 2024 is actually a bit special because that is also when I take COMPE 375, which includes a device for coding that I can use to do quite a few cool things, such as turning the LED on or off or even making sound. The first picture is actually the device that was handed to me for my school assignment. Of course, I also took an Art class, ART 344 Design for the Internet, which I am quite enjoying since that is when I actually learn how to design a website. The middle picture is the practice design I made for my class assignment, while the bottom is my own design for the final project, which is a website to review video games.

SEMESTER 5

Spring 2025

In the fifth semester, I take two COMPE classes at the same time for the first time. The middle picture is the project from COMPE 361, a Python program that simply creates a one-dimensional graph and a three-dimensional version to better visualize the data. The other COMPE class I took is COMPE 470, which is where I learned how to hardware code, basically the code to generate something, and based on that, the machine will create hardware that is able to do exactly that function. The bottom picture is actually the simulation graph for one of my assignments, showing the code's results. The top picture is from ART 544 Emerging Tech Multimedia, where I use Adobe Animate to create an animation that could be launched on the internet. The top picture is a project that plays animation along with sound according to the user, either by clicking or holding the door in a way that is like a website video game.

SEMESTER 6

Spring 2026

Semester 6 is the worst semester I have ever taken at SDSU, and one of the main problems is COMPE 475, which requires a huge amount of work. The middle picture is actually the graphical version of what I've been working on, which is an actual AI that is able to do most things an AI could, like search on the internet or remember a conversation, etc. The top and bottom pictures are all from ART 241, though the top one is my final project, while the bottom one is my practice assignment. That was the first time I learned about Photoshop's mock-up capabilities, which I thought were ridiculously cool, and how convenient it is to view my design as a real product or in real life.
